In addition to my OdorStop system, I also use MERV 11 filters to ensure optimal air quality. The MERV (Minimum Efficiency Reporting Value) rating system measures the effectiveness of air filters in capturing particles. The MERV scale ranges from 1 to 20, with higher ratings indicating better filtration performance. Filters with lower MERV ratings (1-4) are typically designed to capture larger particles like dust and pollen, while higher-rated filters (13-20) can capture smaller particles such as tobacco smoke, bacteria, and viruses. MERV 11 filters fall in the mid-range and are particularly effective at capturing mold spores, pet dander, and pollen.

However, it's essential to choose a filter with a MERV rating appropriate for your HVAC system, as higher MERV ratings result in greater air restriction. Using a filter with a rating that's too high for your system can cause issues such as reduced airflow, increased energy consumption, and even damage to the HVAC system itself. Always consult your HVAC system's manufacturer guidelines to determine the recommended MERV rating for your specific system.

## Maintaining UV Sanitation Systems

To ensure the continued effectiveness of your UV sanitation system, it's essential to replace the UV bulbs regularly. At the very least, UV bulbs should be replaced yearly. However, I have noticed that both GermGuardian and OdorStop bulbs may need replacement after 4-6 months, as they can burn out within this time frame.
