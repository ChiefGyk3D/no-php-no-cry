# Code of Conduct

This project is open to everyone who wants to learn from it or help with it.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions. Everyone starts somewhere.
- Respect privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information, including your
  own more than you mean to.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, threats, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention nobody asked for.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, or promotion that has nothing to do with the project.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, comments and any space where someone
speaks for the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and
block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer
through the GitHub profile linked from the repository, or open an issue that asks
for a private channel and gives no details.
